Our story

What began as distribution

Established in 1997 by Mathew K. Samuel, Everlight Group has grown into a trusted name in the construction supplies industry. What began with a strong foundation in paint, electrical and sanitary ware distribution has expanded from wholesale to retail. Today we supply over 400 shops and serve customers directly through our own showrooms.

We offer a seamless blend of ordinary and premium products under one roof, so every customer, whatever the budget, finds the right fit.
